# Audience and voice

Copy this file to your working project as `audience.md` if you want reusable account context. It is optional; you can also describe the audience in your request.

## Audience

People building small software products and sharing what they learn.

## Voice

Plain English, casual, curious, and specific. Light humor when it fits. Ask useful questions rather than adding generic praise.

## Boundaries

Do not invent personal experiences, imply a relationship with the author, or promote a product without being asked. Use actual details supplied in the current request.
