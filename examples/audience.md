# Audience and voice

Copy this file to your working project as `audience.md` if you want reusable account context, then replace the prompts below with your preferences. It is optional; you can also describe the audience in your request.

## Audience

Describe who you want to reach, their interests, and what makes a conversation relevant to them.

## Voice

Specify your preferred language, tone, level of detail, and use of humor or jargon. You can include a short sample of your own writing. Do not assume a language or persona from these template prompts.

## Boundaries

Do not invent personal experiences, imply a relationship with the author, or promote a product without being asked. Use actual details supplied in the current request.
